TERM AND CONDITION  
	 To be oﬃcially registered as a prospective participant, a payment of 30% of 
the total training price is required, due to limited slot up to 6 participants. This is a 
shared commitment between the Student and Yin Yoga World regarding the 
training program. 
	 An early accepted registration guarantees the Student’s enrollment and 
provided the threshold minimum number of students is reached by the registration 
deadline, ensures the training proceeds as scheduled. 
	 The balance, if any, will be due and payable as stipulated, typically 30 days 
prior to the program's start. To receive the early bird registration fee, the entire 
enrollment fee must be paid in full 45 days before the training begins. 
	 The amount of money we receive is according to the price of TTC. This means 
that all money transfer costs are borne by prospective TTC participants.
CANCELLATION POLICY 
1.Once registered, and any cancellation 45 days prior the training, we will 
refund your advance deposit to your appointed account. 
2.Cancellation between 45 and 30 days prior the training, you will forfeit 50% of 
the total training fee. But if you only make a deposit, the deposit is declared 
forfeited.
3.Cancellation less than 30 days from the training schedule or if the participant 
is unable to continue the training, you will lose 100% of the total registration 
fee.
4.Eligible refunds will be processed within a maximum of 30 days from the date 
of notiﬁcation approval.
