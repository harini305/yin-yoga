TERM AND CONDITION  
•Program Fees and Payment 
Our teacher training programs require advance deposit 30% from total 
amount, for the full fee to be paid to hold a registration due to limited slot up 
to 15 participants. This is a shared commitment between the Student and Yin 
Yoga World regarding the training program. An early accepted registration 
guarantees the Student’s enrollment and provided the threshold minimum 
number of students is reached by the registration deadline, ensures the 
training proceeds as scheduled. 
The balance, if any, will be due and payable as stipulated, typically 30 days 
prior to the program's start. To receive the early bird registration fee, the 
entire enrollment fee must be paid in full 30 days before the training begins. 
The amount of money we receive is according to the price of TTC. This 
means that all money transfer costs are borne by prospective TTC 
participants. 
•Cancellation policy
1.Once registered, and any cancellation 30 days prior the training, we will 
refund your advance deposit to your appointed account. 
2.Cancellation between 30 and 15 days prior the training, you will forfeit 
50% of the total training fee. 
3.Cancellation less than 15 days from the training schedule or after the 
program's start, you will forfeit 100% of the total enrollment fee. 
4.Should you require to withdraw from an ongoing program due to illness, 
accident, or other circumstances that make it impractical for the Student 
to complete the course, Yin Yoga World will provide a reasonable and 
fair refund based on factors such as prior attendance, program length, 
training materials provided, etc. 
5.Eligible refunds will be processed within 30 days of approved notice.
