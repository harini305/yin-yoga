The universe provides unlimited space for creativity. But there are rules that tucked in there. Even the planets also rotate according to their rules, where the stability of the movement will cause gravity. This action gives humans the opportunity to be able to stand on earth and move according to the rhythm of the universe.






Don't be afraid to move, because in fact we are never still. This movement also includes the mental realm. Letting go the past and create hope for the future is one type of mental movement. Move according to the rhythm of the universe.






Take off your burden. Lighten your steps and continue your struggle. Be a complete human being, which means human who only has one highest goal, namely God. And you will be able to survive, stand strong, and be the light like the twinkling stars above.
