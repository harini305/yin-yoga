Yin Yoga Teacher Training Home TTC200 Bali Batch#1 2025




Let us sit close and begin with a light-hearted story. Setting aside, for a moment, the formality and small talk that we so often encounter out there. This little YYW home is a place to learn, to rest, and to foster closeness when we grow weary of the noise and drama beyond its walls. As if the universe is gently reminding us of the importance of connection to rebuild trust that may have faded.




Here, no one feels smart or foolish. Even our lead teacher, Bu Yuni, often says, “I’m sorry if I can’t answer all the questions, because I’m still learning too.” We’ve agreed to take time to practice letting go of both superiority complexes and the inferiority issues that hinder our growth.




We are here not just to earn a certificate. We are here to rediscover a sense of hope that once dimmed under the weight of too many burdens. The dense materials gradually transform into an addiction, one that helps melt the rust from our minds. We become more confident, because here, every potential is appreciated. We believe that the love which decorates our learning space can revive the effort to improve ourselves.




Day by day, time passes. And with every step, we send a prayer, may we all be strengthened until the very end.

Namaskar.
