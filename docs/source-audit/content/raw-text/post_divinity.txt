Divinity
Made Agus
Jan 23, 2024
1 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet





I greet the sun in the morning,

and I feel warmth all over my body.

I enjoy my small steps that bring me closer to the altar.




I feel peace and calmness surround me.

In the corner of the room, my steps stop. 

Suddenly I remember the people who had hurt me.

I feel pity for a moment, and then slowly disappears.




I sit still accompany with the roar of the wind,

and I feel dry leaves drop to the earth.

I find myself swim deeper, and deeper, to my heart and soul...




One by one, I meet all beautiful souls who cross my path.




I begin to greet them one by one.

They are the teachers of the universe, my light.

I'm grateful for the blessings given to me today, tomorrow and who knows how long.

Only He knows.




Day 8: Yin Yoga Teacher Training 200hrs
