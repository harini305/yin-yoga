Intelligence and The Ego Overshadows It
Made Agus
Jan 22, 2024
2 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet







In our lead teacher Yuni's class, she often mentions the relationship of human body organs to certain diseases based on TCM (Traditional Chinese Medicine), include emotions and intelligence. For example, stomach ulcers are usually triggered by stress. Or a heart attack can also be triggered by overjoy feeling. "Intelligence" is much influenced by the spleen which collaborates with the heart to create good brain conditions. However, if you feel less intelligent, it's not certain that the above conditions are the only cause. The kidney also contributes to carrying the essence of energy inherited from parents.




For me, being smart or not being smart is not a reason to fail in a career. Many of those who are successful only rely on little intelligence they have, and then they balance it with diligent work. What we actually need to know is what work suits on our intelligence and emotional level.






Open your eyes, there are many smart people fail because they don't have determination to realize their ideas. Laziness is a factor that hinders success. People who pretend "smart" will tend to have a high ego, they feel other people are stupider than them.




That's why we need to balance intelligence and emotional management. Practicing yoga is one way to achieve that harmony. So, never feel inferior if you are not intelligent enough. Keep learning and balance it with persistence to achieve success.




Bruce Lee said:

”Never underestimate your abilities and say bad words about yourself. Even at that time it was said as a joke. That will make things worst for you.”






Happy learning, I hope you can absorb and understand the knowledge very well.

Let's fight together, Na'ma'ska'r.
