Small Things Matter.
Made Agus
Jan 29, 2024
2 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet





Even though heavy rain poured  in the morning, it didn't dampen my enthusiasm to prepare the equipment needed for the TTC class. Set the whiteboards, tables, seats and also arrange yoga props. Then I noticed that there are 2 spots of water leaking from the roof, and I started to deal with them, so that the participants wouldn't slip while passing.

 

I’m happy and enjoy my work, whatever it is, as long as it provides benefits. Just that. Not every day that we get to do things we like, or do big things that seem spectacular. There are times when we do small things that can lift our mood.




And after the TTC started, I started looking for ideas. What else can I do? Then I quickly put my tele lens on my camera and started hunting. This is my next favorite thing to do.




Chasing butterflies with their dynamic movements, suddenly they’re taking a quick turn, and made me “frustrated in a smile”. Then I start looking for other objects. Then I glance at Manis our lovely studio cat posing on the floor. Ah, too easy! I don't want to photograph objects that are not challenging.




Then I step into the yoga studio to find more interesting objects. And again, without maximum effort LOL. And while walking out, I noticed a small flower that had been neglected. The flower seems calling me, and I immediately want to greet it. Through the deadly shot of my beloved 70-200 lens, enough is enough! This time I felt successful.




Whatever it is, don't wait for a golden opportunity. There are times when small things can actually make your day beautiful and make a happy smile. Happy Monday everyone.
