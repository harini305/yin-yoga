Be Humble And Enjoy Your Meal
Made Agus
Jun 5, 2024
2 min read

Updated: Jun 7, 2024

Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet





Have you ever thought that we are between two worlds that seem to be separate? But, we are connected. Something that we once hated, even blasphemed, but one day maybe we have to live with it? That's a human emotion, sometimes the ego that wraps it up is too thick.

Darkening the bright path that was ready to lead to the gate of light. 




There're always thousands of rejections to bear one achievement.

For example, the AI (Artificial Intelligence). Surprised discoveries, praised and blasphemed. However, the power of AI continues to penetrate, innovate and expand.




The big question is: Do we reject something we don't really understand? 




From long time ago, I have often heard that Yin Yoga is often underestimated. It is said that yin yoga style is just lying around, and some say as yoga that is only suitable for older people or people who are lazy to move. Not innovative. Doesn't make you sweat. Yoga style that is very easy to do, just 1 movement and hold the pose for 3 minutes and you don't need to take part in TTC. And so on... They are busy judging without wanting  to learn completely what is Yin Yoga.




It's sad, but we know that's a natural maturation process. As long as we believe that we need a type of practice that is future-oriented, healthy, a practice that always prioritizes self-balance, then that's actually the kind of yoga we need. Yin yoga is yoga that takes the authentic philosophy of the past and reinvents its script in the present. As simple as that.




Yin yoga, even though it's scorned, but its offerings are always awaited. Like a new dish that brings the appetite for those who really miss yogi-style cooking. A special dish that combines two hidden pearls of India and China. Before enjoying yin yoga, remember one message from chef, be humble and enjoy your meal.
