Blank Space.
Made Agus
Jan 30, 2024
1 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet





After crossing the little path that lead me into my working room,

my steps stopped right in the middle of the room

I looked outside, through the glass window

which is still blurry with dew

I looked at the objects surround me one by one




Everything seems indifferent without meaning

And in that corner my laptop screen was already on

greeted and seduced me with my favorite music




And I grabbed my block and sat.

I'm thinking in silence, my mind wandered




There are doubts filling my head

about an unsolved question

Ah... then I stood back up while muttering

Today is uncertain, i need to be patient.

and I stepped into the kitchen

to enjoy my favorite cake




I realized,

not all days pass smoothly

not all questions need to be answered today

Maybe it's time to rest your mind

and enjoy the remaining time that we have.




FYI: Today I finished 3 portions of my favorite snacks. Can you guess my favourite snack?
