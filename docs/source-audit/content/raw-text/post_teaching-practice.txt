After going through several dramas yesterday which were quite "funny", today we gather again. This morning was quite bright and I notice one by one TTC participants' face. Seems like they are quite ready to do teaching practice.




As usual, the last day of each module will be filled with teaching practice. Not to determine the graduation, but to inspire enthusiasm for teaching and sharing. And the most important is the feedback by lead teacher Yuni.




We often are not aware of our own strengths and weaknesses. It seems like we always need someone who is able to see that and convey it objectively so that in the future we can be better and sharper in teaching.




You need to believe that criticism given with love and affection will be able to bring out goodness and improve talents that may have been hidden.




Enjoy your practice! Believe me, one day you will definitely miss this moment of togetherness.

Na'ma'ska'r.
