Be Precious

1 min read

This is our last day studying together. There is an affective feeling and also feeling proud at the same time for what we have achieved today, and it's the hard work result of a solid team. After stepping foot out there, we become friends who are always willing to open ourselves to share. We will be a family who always wants to support each other and make significant progress together.




Let the beautiful stories we have been through, even silliness become a part of memory that would be a shame to throw away. Thank you for being willing to be an arrow that is ready to fly and hopefully be able to hit its target.




Happy sharing sincerely. Work hard! Honor yourself by keeping your thoughts, words and actions pure. Never make yourself down by talking bad about other people. And success will wait you. Na'ma'skar.
