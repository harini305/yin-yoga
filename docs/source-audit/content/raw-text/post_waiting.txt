We will complete the 200 hour TTC in the next 3 days. There was a little tired look on their faces, but I also felt something else, enthusiasm!




Yes, it is true that studying intensively about new knowledge is not easy. Also, you are required to understand and apply it directly in teaching practice. But with a good approach, I'm sure it will all be easier. Yuni as the lead teacher is able to nurture and guide her students to study in a relaxed way. She doesn't put a lot of pressure, but prioritizes affection.




So far,all students are in good health. Hopefully the next few days that are still left can be passed well.  Keep your spirit alive!
