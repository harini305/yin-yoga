Sharing is liberation.
Made Agus
Oct 14, 2025
1 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet



For often it's the weight of our own anxieties, guilt, and judgments that slowly consume us. What good is abundance if our hearts remain unfulfilled?




Meditation sets the soul free, like a bird soaring through the skies. Don't let greed build walls around your heart. Instead, connect with those who suffer, for in their darkness lies the opportunity for you to find peace, and for them to find hope.




From Yin Yoga TTC200 Hrs - Bali (Sept 28 - Oct 25, 2025)
