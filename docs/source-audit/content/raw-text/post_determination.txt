For some teacher training students, the material from Yin Yoga World is something new. Let's say Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Fascia Anatomy, Meridian Nidra, Wall Yin and many more new things. Salute, because they always learn with enthusiasm!




Also, for the host of Swastam Yoga, this was the first time they run a 200 hours Teacher Training. The building just finished, and also they have some improvements here and there. This is also something new for Yin Yoga World. This is the first time we do a full 200 hour Teacher Training for almost 1 month, offline.




Everything has its own challenges. Many things made us smile, and many more things opened our eyes that all of these require thorough preparation. Thankfully, we have prepared everything quite well. And we are grateful to have a solid and hardworking team so there no drama.




Yuni, as our lead teacher have taught alone for 20 days but with good self-management, she was able to maintain her health until this day. She's very highly disciplined and committed to contributing the best to the progress of her students. Anna and Swastam Bali team are always ready to carry out their duties as a host. And Mey Lan always makes time to stop by in the afternoon after office work, to make sure everything is going well.




I think they all deserve to be awarded. And I feel lucky to be among beautiful souls who are always positive, agile and happy to do their job.




Thank you is all I can say. Only one day left friends, may goodness come from all corners. Na'ma'skar.
