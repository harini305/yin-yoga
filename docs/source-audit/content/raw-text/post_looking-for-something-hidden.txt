Looking for Something Hidden

***A morning reflection.




Humans tend to have attachments to things, because humans are the most thinking and emotional creatures. Two main weapons as a human. Attachment to things, to people or even to the body we have, all the same, is attachment. This creates fear. Fear of having their favorite stolen, fear of losing their girlfriend, fear of looking physically ugly, fear of getting old...and so on.




This situation can be categorized into the genre of attachment. All of this has happened because humans have thoughts, logic and emotions. Being attached means a condition when we feel we need that, and we have to defend it. That's a simple logic. 

But the problem is when you force the will that "I have to have this, I have to defend that... no matter how",  which in fact everythings is impermanent or temporary. That's the human ego.




This is where spiritual knowledge comes into play. Science that is able to balance logic and consciousness. Logic is how our minds process the pile of information we receive, and find the best way to decide something. This can differ from one person to another. But awareness is a hidden power that already exists in the heart, is a divine whisper.




One more that becomes trending nowadays is emotion. Emotions are something that can weaken logic and distance consciousness. Emotions are a powerful form of destructive energy. Emotional states can disrupt the logical order. Often it causes damage, but sometimes emotions produce good results, because they don't depend on analysis and logic. Emotions are spontaneous, random, without careful calculation. Sometimes this is the deciding factor at the finish line of a struggle, destruction or success! But it's always full of risks.




So, are logic, thoughts and emotions necessary?

Of course it's necessary.

But, the most important thing is to use all of them in the right amount, which is balanced

We don't need to glorify it too much, but remember that all of these are just tools or weapons given to us by God to fight the ego. 




True victory is when we are able to stand firmly on Divine consciousness.
