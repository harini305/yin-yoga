New World, New Friends

For those who already know us, they understand the connection between teacher and our students is not tense and formal. We always be your family. Full of jokes when meet, but serious in class. We want the students to feel relax during the learning process, so the knowledge can be well received.


No drama in class. So, be who you are and never underestimate yourself. You are capable of more than you can ever imagine. With a tight study duration, it doesn't even cross into our minds to cause unnecessary problems.


Anything that can make the teaching process also the learning process easier, we take it, we put forth on it. Don't waste your time and energy. Don't let your efforts be in vain.


As our lead teacher Yuni said, "In class there is no graduation test, only teaching practice. The real test is when you teach a real class.”


Once again, hopefully in the next 4 weeks we will be given health, enthusiasm, determination to study and discipline. Because this is our asset for sharing yoga. Be the best version of yourself. The universe is full of blessings, Na'ma'skar.


Swastam Yoga Bali, January 14, 2024
