RYS YIN YOGA WORLD

Yin Yoga World was founded in 2013 after Nyoman Wahyuni E-RYT500 or known as Yuni completed Yin Yoga Teacher Training by Jo Phee at Yinspiration. Then in June 2017 it was registered with Yoga Alliance USA. From 2017 until now Yin Yoga World actively provides workshops and teacher training in Indonesia. During the pandemic time, we had more classes online. Then, we start TTC and Offline Workshops in 2023. Currently, we have RYS200 and RYS300. 

This means, if you take the two programs above, students are eligible to be registered as RYT500 from Yoga Alliance. Under RYS Yin Yoga World, we also do TTC Wheel Yoga and TTC Qi Gong. Everything is based on Functional Yoga. We hope in the years to come we can continue to share both locally and globally.  Google Review

Nyoman Wahyuni E-RYT500
Yuni - Lead Teacher

 

Yuni was first introduced to yoga 20 years ago. Then, her focus was merely a simple asana practice that supported the meditation practice. With a desire to be a teacher, Yuni took up various yoga teacher trainings, including Tantra, Hatha and Prenatal Yoga and has been teaching yoga since 2007.

 

In 2012, Yuni attended a Yin Yoga workshop, and instantly fell in love with it. The following year, she embarked on the Yinspiration 200 hours Yin Yoga teacher training in Ubud, Bali. It was an eye opening experience for her. The teacher training made her realize her love for the quiet and meditative practice of Yin. Since, she has taught Yin Yoga not only in Bali but all over Indonesia.

 

Yuni has completed the 300hrs Speciality program with Yinspiration and is a 500 hours “Certified Yinspiration Teacher”. She regularly assists Jo Phee in her trainings and is currently  serving as a Mentor Teacher for Yinspiration.

 

Yuni resides in Bali with her family but actively travels all of Indonesia to deliver Yin Yoga Teacher Trainings and workshops.

Frequently Asked Question

Does this TTC program include accommodation?

No. You can book a homestay or hotel close to the Yoga Studio/Venue. Very easy to find, because our location is located at tourism area. And you can use online transportation (Gojek or Grab) to reach Yin Yoga World.

 

Will I get a Yoga Alliance certificate?

Certificate will be given by RYS Yin Yoga World. And if you wish to register Yoga Alliance International (USA), then you can use the certificate as a reference. The cost of registering with Yoga Alliance is completely borne by you.

 

Does this TTC have to be taken Full Offline? 

It's best to do our TTC Full Offline. However, due to certain reasons and if you're not being able to do that, an alternative is provided, namely a combination with the zoom recording class. Prices will adjust.

 

Is this TTC entirely in English?

The language used depends on the type of participant. If there are participants who don't speak Indonesian (foreigners), we will use all English. Alternatively, if all participants speak Indonesian, we will use Indonesian. We don't use two languages ​​simultaneously, and we also don't use translators.

 

Is this School accredited with Yoga Alliance?

Yes, RYS Yin Yoga World is a Registered Yoga School (RYS) with Yoga Alliance, which designates this teacher training program as one which follows Yoga Alliance’s standards. click here

 

What time do the classes start and how many days per week?

Our class will start 8am and end at 6pm. In the 22 days program,

we will do classes 5 days a week. Every weekend there are 2 day off (holiday), every Saturday and Sunday. You can use it to rest,

sightseeing, take a break and enjoy the beauty of Bali.

 

Can you explain the rundown?

Yes, please check our example of rundown as follows:

Day 1: 

08.00-10.00: Introduction of Yin Yoga theory

10.00-10.30: break

10.30-12.30: asana lab and practice

12.30-13.45: lunch break

13.45-16.15: Meridian theory

16.15-16.30: short break

16.30-18.00: guided practice

​

Do you have accommodation/ lodging references?

Yes. We are ready to help. Please chat Mey Lan

 

Do I need to bring our own yoga props?

No. We Provide everything including: yoga mat, soft block, yoga rope, bolster, notes, and pen. However, you can bring your favorite yoga mat if you want.

 

How do I pay?

You can make payment by Bank Transfer, Wise or PayPal.
