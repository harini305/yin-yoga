A Touch of the Universe
Made Agus
Feb 11, 2024
2 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet





I remember when I first welcomed you. I feel the burden on your shoulders. You put it here, in this room. As time goes by, we open and untangled these burdens one by one. We make small fragments and learn to understand them.




According to our own karma, it's impossible to reduce or reject the burdens we have to bear in this life. However, the universe always records how far we go to complete it.




Further up there, along with the moon and stars, there's always hope for a change. Believe me, like those planets, life keeps spinning. Move, move and flowing to the rhythm of the rotation, so we don't get left behind. Don't just stay silent. Don't move against the rotation, because you will definitely be left far behind.




Let go of your burden, so we can move faster. Compete to achieve your dreams. Let the sunlight bring its warmth, and let the raindrops shower your body, but never complain. Bring a joy with you.




Remember, this life is a gift. Every journey definitely brings a valuable impression and message. And when you return home, smile, keep your spirit alive and bring your confident.




Don't make yourself inferior. The fire of your passion has been rekindled. The drums are beating. Between the moon and the stars, you are the light. Like a rainbow, you are the brilliance of beauty that colors this life. Your new world. Na'ma'ska'r.




Thank you for our togetherness for 22 days of Teacher Training Yin Yoga. Thank you lead teacher Yuni for your love, for your dedication and passions, all the participants we love, Ibu Anna and Mey Lan. We will miss each other.
