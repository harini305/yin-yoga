Mystical Journey
Made Agus
Apr 30, 2024
1 min read
Rated 0 out of 5 stars.
No ratings yet







When a person is in a calm state, the heartbeat begins to consonance and the veil of the universe begins to be revealed. It was no longer "I" who spoke, but He began to emit vibrations, passing through the dimensions of space and time, carrying universal messages.






Beyond theory and logic, actually there's one thing that is often forgotten,  it's called sense.






Our thoughts give birth to attachment. This is what often clouds our sense. When we are trapped in fear, we often forget what inside there is still Him who has always supported your courage.




The morning before sunrise is a good time to win our sense. Meet Him who always filled with love for you. Awaken those feelings that may have been hidden for so long. Never be afraid to be a "good person" even though it is full of risks. Keep going like a mystical explorer. When that feeling dominates, the power of the universe is truly supporting you.
