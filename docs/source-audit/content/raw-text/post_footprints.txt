I remember the first time I set my feet here, there was a feeling of tension, and even doubt, would I be able to complete this TTC well? With all the efforts and prayers I treasured, I believe in myself, "I can do it!" 

Then I dare to step in.




And the days passed. Tense? Absolutely!

Because the material feels new to me and even some terms still sound not familiar to my ear. With lead teacher Yuni's warm approach, all the material was delivered well. Although to implement it, for me it takes time.




I'm sure, it takes struggle and efforts to understand this new theory and get the benefits of yin yoga practice. But I will make every effort to make it happen. Because I know this is my path.




Today is my last day in Module 1. I want to put all beautiful memories and will engrave them into a long story that is stored deep in my heart.  Hopefully, this gratitude will be a blessing and open my yoga path. And may the universe always bless my steps
