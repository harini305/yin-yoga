Dear Yogiis,

In this digital age, where learning online has become a new culture. Good news, we come with the Yin Yoga TTC 200 HRS Recording Online program. The TTC consists of 4 modules 50 Hours. This recording is presented in English and Indonesia language, made professionally so that it is easy to learn. Each module can be taken separately. But if you want to get an international certificate from Yoga Alliance then you must take all modules to complete the 200 HRS program.

Let's study online!
What is TTC Online Recording

Teacher Training Course conducted online via recording. About 80% of the material 

taught will be recording. You will study yourself through the access we provide, via online. Access given is quite long, around 2-3 months. So you can set the learning time that suits your busy life. And then there will be a meeting to discuss tasks, and discussions about things that 

are not yet understood. This meeting is through live zoom, with a duration of approximately 1 - 3 hours per meeting. For the 50 HRS program, a meeting will be held 2-3 times, and the time will be mutually agreed upon.

​

What are the requirements for following TTC rercording online?

This TTC can be followed by anyone, from all levels of Yoga Practice. And technically it is very easy to do. You only need to prepare gadgets (laptops or even enough mobile phones). And more importantly is you already know Yin Yoga, and have participated in the Yin Yoga class. So that the learning process is easier. You also need to prepare yoga props such as Yoga mat, bolster, soft block (2 pcs) and rope. Just that.

 

Why TTC Recording Online?

Pandemic teaches us many things, including the culture of doing online activities. Of course not everything can be done via online but Yoga Yoga TTC can be done via online, why?

 

The material learned will be conveyed more clearly. You can even play back recording whenever you like.

Learning time is more flexible and longer duration. So you can learn more comfortable. Without the need for extra money for traveling and accommodation, and even you still have income from your job (without having to take day OFF). It makes this course cheaper. And not much energy draining.

You will learn from a trusted and professional yoga school By Yoga Alliance (RYS 200, RYS 300, YACEP) Check here

After you complete this TTC, you still have full access to Teacher.

 

Is this TTC scheduled, or can it be done private?

For now you can follow private 1 on 1 or group. Especially when holding a live zoom meeting.

 

What do you get from this TTC Recording Online?

Guidebook (Module Book)

Access recording for 2-3 months.

E-certificate.

Direct access to teacher via online zoom/ e-mail.

Access to be registered at Yoga Alliance (excluding registration fees).
